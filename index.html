Controls

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD: fly. Space / Alt: rise or dip. Left / right arrows: turn.
  • Drag to look around. Use the mouse wheel to zoom; zoom all the way in for first person, or out to return.
  • Esc: open or close settings.

Controls - Tunnel

  • E: call the butterflies you’ve rescued; nearby companions gather into your flight. Hold the key to let the call gather strength.

Controls - Heaven

  • E: invite nearby butterflies to fly with you, in their own colors.
  • Q: share your color with nearby butterflies, leaving them free.
  • R: let your wings return to white.
  • F: let your companions fly free.

Controller

  • Left stick: fly. Right stick: look around. L2 / R2: dip / rise.
  • Hold R3 and move the left stick up / down to zoom. Zoom all the way in for first person; zoom out to return. Double-tap R3 to switch instantly.
  • Options: open or close settings.

Controls - Tunnel

  • Circle: call the butterflies you’ve rescued; nearby companions gather into your flight. Hold the button to let the call gather strength.

Controls - Heaven

  • Circle: invite nearby butterflies to fly with you, in their own colors.
  • Square: share your color with nearby butterflies, leaving them free.
  • Cross (X): let your wings return to white.
  • Triangle: let your companions fly free.

Touch

  • Left joystick: fly. Right joystick: look around.

Controls - Tunnel

  • Tap your butterfly to call; hold to let the call gather strength. Every call draws the butterflies you’ve rescued into your flight.

Controls - Heaven

  • Tap to make your chosen call. Hold and drag to Gather, Chirp, or White; your last choice stays with you.
  • When companions are with you, choose Release to let them fly free.
